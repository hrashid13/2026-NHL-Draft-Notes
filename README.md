**Top 45 Prospect Scouting Reports**

## **1\. Gavin McKenna**

*LW · Penn State (NCAA) · 5'11", 170 · consensus No. 1 · Comp: Clayton Keller*

**The only player most evaluators consider a potential franchise-changer.**

**Strengths:** Elite, possibly best playmaking, vision and deception; great edgework, escapability and puck control along the wall; controls game pace; was setting up chances at a historic rate for an NCAA draft-year player. Finished 51 pts in 35 games, won the Big Ten scoring title with a great second half.

**Weaknesses:** Plays soft along the boards; perimeter tendencies and quiet even-strength stretches early at Penn State; needs strength; defensive effort has been questioned, but has gotten better; undersized.

**Bottom line:** Slow start created noise, but he answered it. Deals with off ice issues well (WJC noise, off ice incident) which is perfect for Toronto

**Sources:** [McKeen's Hockey](https://www.mckeenshockey.com/nhl-blog/2026-nhl-draft-detailed-scouting-report-gavin-mckenna-lw-penn-state-university-ncaa/)  ·  [ESPN](https://www.espn.com/nhl/story/_/id/48675276/gavin-mckenna-penn-state-expected-no-1-pick-upcoming-nhl-draft)  ·  [Elite Prospects](https://www.eliteprospects.com/news/2026-nhl-draft/whats-going-on-with-gavin-mckenna-at-penn-state)

## **2\. Ivar Stenberg**

*LW/RW · Frölunda HC (SHL) · 5'11", 183 · Sept 2007 · Comp: William Nylander*

**The class's most pro-ready forward; a legitimate 1-vs-2 debate with McKenna.**

**Strengths:** Elite hockey sense and processing; great playmaking and vision second to McKenna although it is close; high-end puck skill; genuinely reliable two-way game and forechecking, which is rare for a top forward. Played vs men, put up 33 pts in 43 SHL games which is the most by an 18-year-old since the Sedins, plus WJC gold and a Champions Hockey League title.

**Weaknesses:** Skating/explosiveness is only average not a burner, struggles to push defenders back; lives on the perimeter at even strength and doesn't get inside consistently; not physical and can shy from contact; many tools are high-end but few are truly elite.

**Bottom line:** The safest high-end bet here, a plug-and-play top-six winger and Calder candidate with a 60–90 point range, maybe slightly lower ceiling great player though. 

**Sources:** [McKeen's Hockey](https://www.mckeenshockey.com/nhl-blog/2026-nhl-draft-detailed-scouting-report-ivar-stenberg-lw-frolunda-shl/)  ·  [Daily Faceoff](https://www.dailyfaceoff.com/news/ivar-stenberg-2026-nhl-draft-profile-scouting-report)  ·  [DobberProspects](https://dobberprospects.com/player/ivar-stenberg/)

## **3\. Caleb Malhotra**

*C · Brantford Bulldogs (OHL) · 6'2", 185 · June 2008 · Comp: Lundell / Beniers / Suzuki*

**The first center likely off the board and the biggest riser among forwards. Son of Manny Malhotra, current Canucks head coach (who have the third overall pick) and nephew of Steve Nash.**

**Strengths:** Elite hockey IQ and 200-foot detail; relentless motor; wins puck battles and works the net-front; processes a step ahead, especially defensively. Exploded for 84 pts (29G) in 67 games as an OHL rookie, then 26 pts in 15 playoff games (13 goals which is 4th-most by an OHL rookie ever).

**Weaknesses:** Average-plus skating with no real separation gear; needs strength; faceoffs are a work in progress; offense is more connective/support-based than dynamic creation, which is fine but a team picking high may need him to great more; production was partly a product of a stacked Brantford team.

**Bottom line:** High-floor, high-character two-way center, the "win games" pick. Going to play at BU next season, will be better competition than the OHL.

**Sources:** [Daily Faceoff](https://www.dailyfaceoff.com/news/caleb-malhotra-2026-nhl-draft-profile-scouting-report)  ·  [Bleacher Report](https://bleacherreport.com/articles/25432237-2026-nhl-mock-draft-caleb-malhotra-scouting-report)  ·  [mynhldraft.com](http://www.mynhldraft.com/2026-nhl-draft/player-profiles/Caleb-Malhotra)

## **4\. Chase Reid**

*D (RH) · Soo Greyhounds (OHL) · 6'2", 190 · Dec 2007 · Comp: Evan Bouchard*

**CHL Top Prospect Award winner with a huge rise from the NAHL.**

**Strengths:** Elite, NHL-ready skating and edges; the best in the class at turning defense into offense; high-end processing and poise under pressure; offensive creativity and PP-QB ability; physical strength and reach. 18G, 48 pts in 35 games despite a wrist injury; strong WJC for USA.

**Weaknesses:** Defensive reads and positioning are a work in progress, chases the play and gets out of position at times but can make up for it in skating at the junior level; some indecision defending rushes; not polished yet.

**Bottom line:** Highest two-way ceiling among the D; MSU-bound next year should be better competition

**Sources:** [McKeen's Hockey](https://www.mckeenshockey.com/nhl-blog/2026-nhl-draft-detailed-scouting-report-chase-reid-d-sault-ste-marie-greyhounds-ohl/)  ·  [Daily Faceoff](https://www.dailyfaceoff.com/news/2026-nhl-draft-chase-reid-top-defenseman-san-jose-sharks-soo-greyhounds)  ·  [Elite Prospects](https://www.eliteprospects.com/player/576955/chase-reid/scouting-report)

## **5\. Carson Carels**

*D (LH) · Prince George Cougars (WHL) · 6'2", 198 · June 2008 · Comp: Werenski / Sanderson*

**Craig Button's pick as the first D off the board; a young, complete two-way blueliner.**

**Strengths:** Elite four-way skating, edgework and explosiveness; genuine compete and a mean streak; physical; quarterbacked PG's power play; very good passer and transporter; can score (20G, 73 pts in 58 games, \~25–28 min/night). Made Canada's WJC team as an underager.

**Weaknesses:** Consistency is the main one; some trackers flag him as flat-footed/inactive in defensive transition; offensively more facilitator than primary creator, which is fine; occasional decision-making lapses.

**Bottom line:** As complete a defenseman as this class offers; top-pair upside. North Dakota-bound next year, the highest floor of the big 3 defenseman. 

**Sources:** [McKeen's Hockey](https://www.mckeenshockey.com/nhl-blog/2026-nhl-draft-detailed-scouting-report-carson-carels-d-prince-goerge-cougars-whl/)  ·  [NHL.com](https://www.nhl.com/news/topic/nhl-draft/chase-reid-carson-carels-keaton-verhoeff-among-best-defensemen-in-2026-nhl-draft)  ·  [The Hockey Writers](https://thehockeywriters.com/carson-carels-2026-nhl-draft-prospect-profile/)

## **6\. Keaton Verhoeff**

*D (RH) · North Dakota (NCAA) · 6'4", 208 · June 2008 · Comp: Ekblad / Dobson*

**The coveted archetype: big right-shot D with offense and one of the youngest players in the draft.**

**Strengths:** Near-elite shot and release; defends the blue line as well as anyone in the class (lots of stick checks); strong gap control, size and poise; offensive-zone vision. Logged big minutes as a 17-year-old freshman (20 pts in 36 games) and made Canada's WJC team underage.

**Weaknesses:** Skating isn't elite (mobile for his size, not dynamic); needs to play at a quicker pace, especially on breakouts; decision-making can get exposed against heavy forechecks (struggled at the Frozen Four); offensive game still raw and production tailed off late.

**Bottom line:** Enormous long-term upside but more of a project than his frame suggests; likely returns to UND. Played against better competition than the other 2 defenseman, and because of this struggled a little more. 

**Sources:** [McKeen's Hockey](https://www.mckeenshockey.com/nhl-blog/2026-nhl-draft-detailed-scouting-report-keaton-verhoeff-d-university-north-dakota-ncaa/)  ·  [Daily Faceoff](https://www.dailyfaceoff.com/news/keaton-verhoeff-2026-nhl-draft-u-18-world-championship-scouting-report-north-dakota)  ·  [Broad Street Hockey](https://www.broadstreethockey.com/post/bsh-2026-community-draft-board-no-4-keaton-verhoeff-can-be-what-every-team-wants/)

## **7\. Viggo Björck**

*C/RW · Djurgården IF (SHL) · 5'10", 172 · March 2008 · Comp: McMichael / Kane (style)*

**Possibly the most overlooked high-ceiling player in the class.**

**Strengths:** Elite hockey IQ, plays two steps ahead; slick, creative playmaking and a pro-level shot; relentless work rate and real two-way commitment; battles and goes to the net despite his size. Became Djurgården's first-line center down the stretch and into the playoffs as a 17-year-old, had a great WJC.

**Weaknesses:** Size (undersized for center, especially a number 1); skating is quick/light rather than explosive — first steps need work; can drift to the perimeter; will likely slide on size/speed concerns.

**Bottom line:** A 60+ point two-way center bet with a high ceiling to be better.

**Sources:** [McKeen's Hockey](https://www.mckeenshockey.com/nhl-blog/2026-nhl-draft-detailed-scouting-report-viggo-bjorck-c-djurgarden-shl/)  ·  [The Hockey Writers](https://thehockeywriters.com/viggo-bjorck-2026-nhl-draft-prospect-profile/)  ·  [Broad Street Hockey](https://www.broadstreethockey.com/post/2026-bsh-community-draft-board-no-6-viggo-bjorck-is-the-most-overlooked-prospect-this-year/)

## **8\. Alberts Smits**

*D (LH) · Jukurit (Liiga) / EHC München (DEL) · 6'3", 205 · Dec 2007 · Comp: Hedman lite*

**The "proof-of-concept" prospect, left Latvia alone at 13 and already played the Olympics, Worlds and pro hockey.**

**Strengths:** Rare size \+ mobility; "skates like the wind"; pro-ready defending; excellent hockey IQ and first passes; leads the rush; good shot; held his own against NHLers at the Olympics (the only draft-eligible player there) and led Latvia's D in ice time at the Worlds. BEAST

**Weaknesses:** Wants a more consistent physical streak; offensive game vs. men is still developing, but it's fine; some debate about competition/sample. Apparently has attitude issues

**Bottom line:** I like him more than others, I see massive potential in him and it is impressive he has played vs men instead of racking up huge totals vs lesser competition. 

**Sources:** [NHL.com](https://www.nhl.com/news/alberts-smits-2026-nhl-draft-stock-on-rise-after-playing-for-latvia-in-olympics)  ·  [All About The Jersey](https://www.allaboutthejersey.com/2026-nhl-draft-prospects/70159/albert-smits-carsen-carels-xavier-villeneuve-2026-left-shot-defensemen-draft-profiles)  ·  [Scouching](https://www.scouching.ca/the-blog/preliminary-2026-ranking)

## **9\. Nikita Klepov**

*RW/F · Saginaw Spirit (OHL) · 6'0", 178 · June 2008 · Comp: Kyle Connor*

**The OHL's scoring leader (97 pts) a gifted playmaker**

**Strengths:** Elite puck skill, playmaking and vision, rivals McKenna and Stenberg; legit power-play skill with little fakes/hesitations; calm and composed; cuts through defenders; improved as a 5-on-5 shooter/finisher this year; competes for loose pucks.

**Weaknesses:** Clunky stride and skater with poor lower-body connection and balance; vulnerable in transition; not physical and needs strength; defensive detail ranges.

**Bottom line:** A skilled scoring winger whose skating is the swing factor; mid-first. MSU-bound next year. 

**Sources:** [All About The Jersey](https://www.allaboutthejersey.com/devils-issues-views-and-ge/69939/nikita-klepov-2026-nhl-draft-prospect-profile-a-gifted-playmaker-with-incredible-puck-skills)  ·  [The Hockey Writers](https://thehockeywriters.com/nikita-klepov-2026-nhl-draft-prospect-profile/)  ·  [ESPN](https://www.espn.com/nhl/story/_/id/48666667/2026-nhl-draft-rankings-top-prospects-players-gavin-mckenna-ivar-stenberg-carson-carels)

## **10\. Daxon Rudolph**

*D (RH) · Prince Albert Raiders (WHL) · 6'2", 205 · Comp: Noah Hanifin*

**A smart, poised right-shot who flew a bit under the radar behind Reid/Verhoeff/Carels.**

**Strengths:** High-end hockey sense and three-zone poise; elite passing and a cannon of a shot; excellent at setting up one-timers; plays a simple, effective game with great feel for pressure. Set a Prince Albert record with 28 goals (78 pts in 68 games) and played a shutdown role internationally for Canada.

**Weaknesses:** Passive (\!\!\!) tends to anticipate and hang back rather than dictate; prefers sealing lanes to leaning on people; not physical. Needs to learn how to maximize his tools at the NHL level

**Bottom line:** Top-four-upside RHD; end-of-top-10 to mid-first range.Not as high potential as the big 3 but a great player. 

**Sources:** [All About The Jersey](https://www.allaboutthejersey.com/2026-nhl-draft-prospects/70304/chase-reid-keaton-verhoeff-daxon-rudolph-ryan-lin-2025-right-shot-defenseman-profiles)  ·  [NHL.com](https://www.nhl.com/news/topic/nhl-draft/chase-reid-carson-carels-keaton-verhoeff-among-best-defensemen-in-2026-nhl-draft)  ·  [ESPN](https://www.espn.com/nhl/story/_/id/48666667/2026-nhl-draft-rankings-top-prospects-players-gavin-mckenna-ivar-stenberg-carson-carels)

## **11\. Tynan Lawrence**

*C · Boston University (NCAA) · 6'0", 185 · Aug 2008 · Comp: Nick Schmaltz*

**A high-end skater whose midseason jump to BU stalled his production.**

**Strengths:** Excellent, elite, explosive skating; reliable two-way game; a "transition machine" (because of his skating) with strong closing speed and a relentless motor; smart connective plays. Dominated the USHL (71 pts in 69 games) and was Muskegon's playoff MVP.

**Weaknesses:** The NCAA jump exposed him, only 7 pts in 18 BU games and it feels he wasn't ready; playmaking needs to be worked on; not physically ready for the pros.

**Bottom line:** Tools say second-line center; a full BU season should help. Wide ranking range. Project player who has an unteachable valuable tool (Skating)

**Sources:** [NBC Sports Philadelphia](https://www.nbcsportsphiladelphia.com/nhl/philadelphia-flyers/nhl-draft-2026-tynan-lawrence-scouting-report-second-line-center-upside/735568/)  ·  [McKeen's Hockey](https://www.mckeenshockey.com/nhl-blog/2026-nhl-draft-detailed-scouting-report-tynan-lawrence-c-boston-university/)  ·  [NHL.com](https://www.nhl.com/news/nhl-mock-draft-after-2026-scouting-combine)

## **13\. Ethan Belchetz**

*LW · Windsor Spitfires (OHL) · 6'5", 227 · March 2008 · Comp: Nichushkin*

**A genuine power-forward "wrecking ball." Wears No. 61 for Rick Nash**

**Strengths:** Massive frame and physical dominance; net-front and forecheck power; goal-scoring instincts (34G, 59 pts in 57 games); improving passing, solid IQ and short agility. A high-volume hitter who impacts almost every shift.

**Bottom line:** A power winger with top-six finishing upside. MSU-bound next year, may get overdrafted because of size and play style
